**The concerns worth knowing about:**

There have been negative reviews, and they deserve mention. During the Black Friday 2025 promotion period, DediRock got hit with a massive wave of new customers and experienced stability issues for some users — the founder/support lead Danny acknowledged this publicly and directly. One user reported a RAID controller failure in February 2026 that led to data loss (DediRock confirmed this was a rare dual-failure hardware event). Another user complained about the management UI being clunky and slow.

Danny's responses to every single negative review are worth reading — they're personal, specific, and non-defensive. That's unusual and actually tells you something about how the company operates.

The takeaway? **DediRock is not a hands-off, enterprise-grade host.** You should not run mission-critical production data without independent backups. But for developers, hobbyists, homelab users, and people who know what they're getting into with budget VPS — it's a genuinely good deal.
